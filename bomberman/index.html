How a stage ends

Every stage hides an exit door under a brick. After you've killed every monster, blow up bricks to find the door, then walk onto it to clear the stage. Bombing the door or an exposed power-up spawns extra monsters — bad idea. Reinforced bricks (stage 50+) take two hits to break.

Stages

25 stages with rising difficulty. Every 3rd stage is a BONUS round — 30 seconds to bomb as many monsters as possible for huge points. Every 5th stage is a BOSS with new tricks. Build a kill streak to multiply your score; your best run is saved on this device.
